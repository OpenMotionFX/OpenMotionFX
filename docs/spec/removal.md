# Removal (digest)

Phase 5 work: taking stands and rigs out of a shot. Status: **not started; nothing here is tested**.

## Build

- Witness phones (second-hand) and, if possible, a P4 witness camera (solo § Phase 5)
- The registry in the set app (solo § Phase 5)
- Infrared tags: retroreflective tape plus an infrared light, seen by a witness with its infrared filter removed, if a witness allows that (solo § Phase 5)
- Fills from other views, the set reconstruction and the slider's clean passes (solo § Phase 5)

## Film test

- A shot with a stand and a rig removed automatically (solo § Phase 5)

## The full-scale idea, for orientation only

- Every object that is not world has a registry entry: scan, material, tag code, live pose; cleanup in post reads this registry (stack § Software stack)
- Units self-register and are flagged when they enter the frustum, a reflection cone or a shadow volume (stack § Rules so the stack)
- The tag band (around 940 nm) gives contaminant mattes without touching visible light (stack § Rules so the stack)
- Never-seen surfaces are still inferred (stack § Fundamentals mapped to modules)

## Rules for any removal

- Every removal comes with its matte, its source views and a switch to turn it off (review § VFX artist)
- A clean plate that is 98% right is worse than a dirty one, because the 2% is hard to see (review § VFX artist)
- A passive mode with no splitter and no codes must still give rig removal (review § Director and cinematographer)
- With few witnesses, say honestly where the system could not see (review § Solo, low to zero budget creator)
- Bystander blur on by default; consent recorded with every actor scan (review § Stakeholder review)
